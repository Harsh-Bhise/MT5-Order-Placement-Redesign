Overview

This system covers the order placement flow of the MT5 redesign: placing an order, its confirmation and the positions list. It is not a full design system for the app. It documents only what the flow uses.

Every component on this page is captured from the working prototype, using the same styles. What you see here is exactly what ships in the prototype.

Risk firstThe trader states the risk. The app does the conversion.
Results lead, inputs followEach card shows what the trader gets, then the control that changes it.
Every number appears onceA value is shown in one place, so nobody has to check that two numbers agree.
Errors explain the fixA blocked order always says why and offers the change that would make it work.

Accessibility standard

WCAG 2.2 level AA, plus Apple's 44 × 44 pt minimum touch target. Color is never the only signal: Buy and Sell carry their words, profit and loss carry + and − signs, and warnings carry an icon. See Accessibility for how this was checked.

Rules

  • Blue and red mean direction and money. Blue is Buy and profit, red is Sell and loss. They are not used for decoration.
  • Supporting lines stay neutral. Money in a supporting line is label-secondary, not red or blue, so the main value keeps focus.
  • Amber is for warnings that do not block the order. Red is for errors that do.

Spacing, radius and elevation

Spacing

A small set, used the same way everywhere.

  • 4 Value to supporting line
  • 8 Between controls in a row, between cards in a list
  • 12 Between groups, supporting line to controls
  • 14 Card side padding
  • 16 Screen margins

Radius

Controls are capsules. Cards share one radius.

  • 7 Chips
  • 22 Cards, steppers, segmented controls
  • 25 Primary button (50 pt tall)
  • 36 Floating sheets

Elevation

Screens are flat: white cards on a grey background, no shadows. Only floating layers are raised, using a translucent "glass" fill and a soft shadow: the navigation buttons, the commit sheet and the tab bar. They float because content scrolls under them.

Order card

The building block of the order screen. Position Size, Stop Loss and Take Profit all use it, so the trader reads every card the same way.

Anatomy

  1. Label row. Card name, with its switch or tag on the right.
  2. Value. The result the trader gets, in Value style.
  3. Supporting line. What the value means, in neutral color.
  4. Controls. Always last, in one row. They wrap to two rows on narrow phones.

States

Specs

Surfacesurface, radius 22, padding 12 top · 14 sides · 14 bottom

Controls12 below the supporting line, 8 between controls
FootnoteCaption 12, outside the card, 16 from the screen edge

Usage

Do

Lead with the result ("0.58 lots") and put the control that changes it underneath.

Don't

Put the control first, or show the same number in two cards.

Accessibility

  • Errors use red plus an icon, and the message is announced as an alert.
  • A warning never blocks the order. Only errors that would make the order fail disable the button.
  • Controls wrap instead of clipping, from a 320 pt wide phone up.

Stepper

For every number the trader sets: risk, lots, stop and target distance, prices. The value can be typed directly or nudged with − and +.

Anatomy

  1. − and + buttons. 44 × 44 pt each. Hold to repeat.
  2. Value field. An optional label, the number and its unit. The number is a text field: tapping it selects the value, ready to retype. The label hides below 205 pt wide, the unit below 150 pt.

States

Specs

Size44 tall, full width of its column
Border1 pt border token, 2 pt red in error
Value16 / 500, tabular figures
StepsRisk 0.25 % or 10 USD, lots 0.01, distances 1 pip, prices exactly 1 pip

Accessibility

  • Each button has a label such as "Increase Stop Loss distance in pips".
  • Works from the keyboard: Tab to a button, Enter or Space to step, Enter in the field to confirm.
  • The field opens the decimal keypad and uses 16 pt text, so iOS does not zoom in.

Segmented controls and switch

Three segmented controls, each for a different weight of decision, plus the standard switch.

When to use which

Direction toggleBuy or Sell. The live price sits on each side, and the selected side fills blue or red.
Unit (% / $)Changes the unit of the stepper beside it. Selected segment fills blue.
Pips / PriceA quieter iOS-style control for how the Stop Loss is entered. Take Profit follows the same unit.
SwitchTurns a whole card on or off: Auto, Stop Loss when Auto is off, Take Profit.

Accessibility

  • Each segment is a button with its pressed state exposed, and a 44 pt tall hit area.
  • The switch is a real switch role with on and off announced. Its hit area extends to 45 pt.
  • The selected direction is shown by fill and by the words Buy and Sell, never by color alone.

Context card

What is being traded and how. Set once and rarely touched, so it stays quiet at the top of the screen.

Specs

RowsMinimum 48 tall, Body 16 title, Caption 12 description
Right sideCaption 12 label-tertiary detail, then a chevron
Entry rowPending orders only. 56 tall, stepper 196 wide

Do

Describe what the order does: "Fills now at the best available price".

Don't

Use the broker's internal term ("Market Execution") as the label.

Commit sheet

The last check before the trade. It floats above the content, so the margin and the button are always visible.

Anatomy

  1. Status line. Margin Used when the order is valid, or the reason it is blocked.
  2. Fix. One tap that applies the change, with its effect next to it.
  3. Primary button. States exactly what it will do. Disabled until the order is valid.

States

Specs

SurfaceGlass, radius 36, 8 from the screen edges
Button50 tall, radius 25, Button 16 / 600. Blue for Buy, red for Sell
Disabledseparator fill, label-tertiary text
SpacingThe content above stops right at the sheet, with no dead space

Do

Write the action in the button: "Buy 0.58 lots at Market".

Don't

Add a confirmation pop-up after a button that already says what it will do.

Position card

One open position or pending order on the Trade tab. Three levels, in the order a trader checks them.

Anatomy

  1. What and how much. Symbol, direction chip and P/L, the largest values.
  2. How it is moving. Entry → current price, and the move in pips.
  3. Protection. After a divider: Stop Loss, Take Profit and the maximum loss.

States

Accessibility

  • The whole card is one button, read as "EURUSD buy 0.58 lots, profit +12.18 USD".
  • Profit and loss carry + and − signs as well as color.
  • The direction chip has its word ("Buy 0.58"), not just a color.
