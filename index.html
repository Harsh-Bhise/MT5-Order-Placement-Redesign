MetaTrader 5 · Order placement redesign

Size every trade by the amount you're willing to lose. The app does the maths, checks margin, and explains every error with a fix.

Try these

  1. Place a trade risking 1% with a 20-pip Stop Loss.
  2. Place a fixed 0.50-lot trade (switch Auto off).
  3. Before buying, find out how much you could lose.
  4. Place a Buy Limit below the market, then find it on the Trade tab.
